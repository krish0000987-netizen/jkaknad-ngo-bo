## 🌟 Executive Overview
Designed to meet the benchmark of a **₹50 Lakh national foundation digital presence**, this comprehensive 10-page web portal represents the verified grassroots social welfare work of **Jharkhandvasi Parivartan Sangh (JPS)**.

Founded in 2013 under the leadership of Founder & National President **Arvind Arya** and National General Secretary **Tulsi Kumar Yadav**, JPS operates as a vital humanitarian bridge between the migrant worker diaspora in Mumbai/Maharashtra and their home communities across Jharkhand (Giridih, Ranchi, Dhanbad, Bokaro).

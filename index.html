Who We Are

Serving the Underserved With Dignity and Compassion

Jharkhandvasi Parivartan Sangh (JPS) is a community-focused non-governmental social welfare organization headquartered in Borivali West, Mumbai. Founded under the leadership of Arvind Arya, JPS was established to address the critical social, economic, and humanitarian challenges faced by migrant workers, vulnerable elders, and marginalized children.

Whether assisting stranded workers during medical emergencies, coordinating child rehabilitation with government Child Welfare Committees (CWC), or organizing free geriatric health camps, our work is driven by one core philosophy: "समाज के लिए, समाज के साथ" (For the society, with the society).

Guiding Principles

The Foundation of Our Social Impact

Every initiative we undertake is anchored in transparency, empathy, and collective community empowerment.

Our Mission

To provide unconditional 24/7 crisis support, legal guidance, and healthcare assistance to migrant communities, while fostering child literacy and senior citizen welfare through sustained grassroots programs.

Our Vision

A society where every migrant worker lives with equal rights and dignity, every elderly citizen receives respected healthcare, and no child is deprived of education or safety.

Our Core Values

सेवा (Service), एकता (Unity), संघर्ष (Perseverance), and पारदर्शिता (Integrity). We treat every individual seeking help as family, ensuring compassionate intervention without discrimination.

24/7 Crisis Intervention

Migrant Distress Response & Dignified Repatriation

When an underprivileged migrant worker faces sudden medical trauma, industrial death, or acute destitution in Mumbai, families hundreds of kilometers away in Jharkhand often have nowhere to turn. JPS operates a direct grassroots emergency protocol:

  • ✓ 24/7 emergency response team arrives at Mumbai hospitals or police jurisdictions.
  • ✓ Administrative coordination for no-objection paperwork, embalming, and transport.
  • ✓ Repatriation of mortal remains to native ancestral villages with utmost dignity.
  • ✓ Representation with District Magistrates and local administrators for compensation.

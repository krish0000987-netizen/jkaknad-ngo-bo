Our Identity & Heritage

Who We Are

Built upon solidarity, relentless grassroots service, and the principle of "एक ज़रूरत • एक प्रयास • एक बदलाव" — serving communities across Mumbai and Jharkhand since 2013.

Genesis & Evolution

Born From Grassroots Need in Mumbai

In the bustling economic metropolis of Mumbai, hundreds of thousands of people from Jharkhand work tirelessly as laborers, security guards, hospitality workers, artisans, and transport operators. Often far from home, these families faced an acute void: whenever a medical crisis, accidental death, unlawful wage withholding, or family distress occurred, they had neither institutional representation nor an immediate community emergency safety net.

In 2013, founder Arvind Arya and dedicated social workers established Jharkhandvasi Parivartan Sangh (JPS) in Borivali West, Mumbai. The mission was clear: create an uncompromising 24/7 support system that ensures no migrant feels alone in Maharashtra, while expanding into child literacy, elder health, and cultural heritage preservation.

"सेवा केवल एक कार्य नहीं, जीवन का संकल्प है। जब कोई प्रवासी या असहाय व्यक्ति संकट में होता है, तो त्वरित सहायता ही सच्ची मानवता है।"

— अरविंद आर्य (संस्थापक एवं राष्ट्रीय अध्यक्ष)

The Social Reality

Why Our Work Matters

Addressing four deep-seated community vulnerabilities in urban and semi-urban ecosystems.

🛡️

Protection for Migrant Workers

Overcoming language barriers, lack of legal assistance, arbitrary wage deductions, and absence of emergency hospitalization backing when migrant laborers face life-threatening crises.

❤️

Dignity for Senior Citizens

Elderly residents in low-income urban clusters often lack routine health screenings, hypertension/diabetes diagnostics, and dignified emotional and community support.

📚

Educational Hope for Children

Intervening against child labor through Child Welfare Committee (CWC) coordination and equipping underprivileged children with notebooks, bags, and learning kits.

Strategic Roadmap

Empowering Every Individual Through Dignified Action

Our Mission

To operate as a round-the-clock humanitarian shield for migrant workers and vulnerable families in Maharashtra and Jharkhand; providing free medical assistance, child rehabilitation, elder care, and cultural preservation without bureaucratic delay.

Our Vision

An inclusive, socially conscious nation where geographic migration does not strip an individual of their human dignity, where elderly citizens are cherished, and where our tribal and regional heritage is celebrated with pride.

Ethical Anchors

Our Organizational Principles

Values that govern our volunteers, leadership, and public interventions.

🤝

करुणा (Compassion)

Treating every family facing distress with unconditional empathy, understanding their lived realities, and offering immediate emotional and material comfort.

🕊️

सेवा (Selfless Service)

Approaching social work not as philanthropy, but as our civic and moral duty toward our brothers, sisters, and elders in need.

⚖️

निष्ठा (Integrity & Truth)

Strict transparency in public affairs, zero false claims, and unwavering honesty with beneficiaries, authorities, and supporters.

🌐

समावेशिता (Inclusion)

Extending aid to every person regardless of caste, creed, religion, or background. Humanity and suffering know no boundaries.

👥

समुदाय (Community Solidarity)

Believing in collective strength. When people unite under shared heritage and goals, genuine societal transformation follows.

📋

उत्तरदायित्व (Responsibility)

Following through on every crisis call to its conclusion — from emergency hospital admission to safe recovery or dignified repatriation.

Milestones

Our Journey of Transformation

Over a decade of continuous service, grassroots expansion, and administrative advocacy.

2013

Foundation in Borivali West

Jharkhandvasi Parivartan Sangh founded under Arvind Arya in Mumbai to create a formal mutual aid network for migrant workers from Jharkhand.

2016

Senior Citizen Health Initiatives

Initiated regular free geriatric diagnostic and health checkup camps in Borivali and northern Mumbai suburbs, providing free consultations and basic medicines.

2019

Child Welfare Committee (CWC) Collaboration

Began structured coordination with CWC and local administration to rehabilitate children rescued from exploitative labor and distribute school starter kits.

2020 – 2021

COVID-19 Humanitarian Relief

Operated dry ration drives, delivered meals, arranged emergency medical admissions, and coordinated safe transit for stranded daily-wage workers during pandemic lockdowns.

2023

District Administration Delegations

Official JPS delegations engaged with District Magistrates / Deputy Commissioners in Jharkhand (Giridih, Ranchi) to formally address migrant welfare policies and safety nets.

2024 – 2026

Expanded 24/7 Helpline & Digital Network

Established an active 24/7 emergency phone and digital network reaching over 5,200+ members, supporting migrant families with instant crisis response.

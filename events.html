Bhagwan Birsa Munda Smruti Sohala

Annual cultural commemoration and remembrance honoring Dharti Aaba Bhagwan Birsa Munda. Featuring traditional floral offerings, brass deep lighting, and inspiring address on tribal rights and unity.

Migrant Labor Rights & Welfare Consultation

Grassroots convention under Arvind Arya addressing safety protocols, grievance submission procedures, inter-state administrative desks, and emergency accident response networks.

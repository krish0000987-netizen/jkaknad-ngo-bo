Join a brotherhood of compassionate changemakers. Once you submit this form, our volunteer desk will connect with you via WhatsApp / Phone to assign you to upcoming community sessions.

What Volunteers Do at JPS:

  • ✓ Manage attendee registration at Free Senior Citizen Medical Camps.
  • ✓ Coordinate book distribution and student mentoring sessions.
  • ✓ Assist in logistical packaging of emergency dry ration packets.
  • ✓ Help monitor and respond to distress calls from migrant families.
  • ✓ Support cultural commemoration events and unity conventions.

Programs & Field Action

Where Compassion Becomes Action

Discover our six focused pillars of social intervention, designed to protect rights, restore health, educate children, and preserve community dignity.

24/7 Helpline

Immediate Crisis & Hospitalization Assistance

Pillar 01

Migrant Worker Welfare, Crisis Aid & Shav Repatriation

Migrant workers in Mumbai frequently lack local emergency safety nets. When sudden medical emergencies, wage withholding, or workplace fatalities strike, JPS acts as an immediate on-the-ground guardian.

Key Activities:

  • ✓ Mortal Remains Repatriation: Completing hospital/police NOC formalities and coordinating respectful transfer of deceased workers back to native villages in Jharkhand.
  • ✓ Emergency Hospitalization: Accompanying injured workers to civic hospitals, advocating for timely medical treatment and trauma care.
  • ✓ Wage & Exploitation Redressal: Engaging with employers and labor desks to recover withheld wages and resolve disputes.
  • ✓ Legal Aid Consultation: Guiding unrepresented laborers on basic legal rights and compensation.
Who it helps: Daily-wage laborers, security personnel, construction workers, and domestic migrant families residing in suburban Mumbai and Thane.

Pillar 02

Senior Citizen Health, Dignity & Elder Care

Elderly citizens in underserved urban communities face chronic, undiagnosed conditions such as hypertension, diabetes, arthritis, and diminishing eyesight. JPS provides continuous geriatric health support.

Key Activities:

  • ✓ Free Geriatric Screening Camps: Regular blood pressure, blood glucose, and vitals checkups conducted by verified doctors in Borivali West.
  • ✓ Vision Screenings: Eye checkups and coordination for spectacles for seniors with failing vision.
  • ✓ Nutritional & Medicine Subsidies: Providing basic prescribed vitamins and essential medications to elderly patients in need.
  • ✓ Companionship & Care Visits: Volunteers checking in on isolated seniors to ensure emotional wellbeing and safety.
Who it helps: Vulnerable grandmothers, grandfathers, and elderly couples with limited family financial support living in Mumbai's western suburbs.

Empowering the Next Generation

Pillar 03

Child Protection, Rehabilitation & Education Support

Children of migrant and low-income families are especially vulnerable to discontinuing school and falling into informal labor. JPS intervenes actively to keep every child in school.

Key Activities:

  • ✓ CWC Coordination: Working alongside the government Child Welfare Committee (CWC) for safe rehabilitation and schooling of rescued children.
  • ✓ School Kit Drives: Distributing robust school bags, notebooks, pencils, crayons, and geometry boxes to primary school students.
  • ✓ Parent Counseling: Meeting migrant parents to emphasize the crucial value of unbroken schooling for both boys and girls.
  • ✓ Remedial Study Circles: Volunteer-guided study groups helping children complete school homework and basic literacy skills.
Who it helps: Children of migrant laborers, urban slum youth, and rescued minors seeking dignified rehabilitation into formal schooling.

Pillar 04

Free Healthcare Camps & Blood Donation Drives

Preventive healthcare is often a luxury that daily-wage families cannot afford until a minor symptom turns catastrophic. JPS brings preventive care directly into community halls.

Key Activities:

  • ✓ Multi-Specialty Camps: General physicians, pediatricians, and ophthalmologists providing free consultations.
  • ✓ Free Medicine Distribution: Prescribed analgesics, antacids, vitamins, and ointments supplied at zero cost.
  • ✓ Voluntary Blood Donation Drives: Mobilizing community youth and donors to support civic blood banks for accident victims.
  • ✓ Health & Hygiene Workshops: Educating migrant colonies on water purification, monsoon sickness prevention, and sanitation.
Who it helps: Marginalized households, migrant settlements, and daily-wage families who have no health insurance or regular medical access.
Request a Health Camp View Camp Photos
Pillar 05

Women Empowerment & Family Crisis Relief

Migrant women bear the heaviest burden during economic disruptions. JPS stands beside mothers, daughters, and families in acute distress with both immediate emergency aid and self-reliance guidance.

Key Activities:

  • ✓ Emergency Dry Ration Kits: Distributing flour, rice, pulses, and cooking oil to families facing wage suspension or illness.
  • ✓ Family Dispute & Distress Counseling: Neutral community elders resolving marital or domestic crises with empathy.
  • ✓ Maternal Guidance: Nutritional education and hospital delivery support for expectant mothers.
  • ✓ Self-Help Networking: Connecting skilled women with local tailoring, culinary, or artisanal work avenues.
Who it helps: Single mothers, widows of deceased migrant workers, and low-income families navigating urban economic precarity.
Support Family Relief Connect With Team
Pillar 06

Cultural Heritage, Martyr Tributes & Social Unity

True social empowerment is incomplete without cultural pride. JPS keeps the glorious revolutionary legacy and traditional festivals of Jharkhand alive in the heart of Mumbai.

Key Activities:

  • ✓ Bhagwan Birsa Munda Commemoration: Annual tribute ceremonies honoring the iconic tribal freedom fighter with floral garlanding, lighting of brass lamps, and inspirational seminars.
  • ✓ Honoring Tribal Martyrs: Commemorating heroes such as Veer Tilka Manjhi, Sidho-Kanho, and Nilamber-Pitamber to educate youth on historic struggles for justice.
  • ✓ Traditional Festivals: Community gatherings for Karam Puja and Sarhul, bringing together families across Maharashtra.
  • ✓ Diaspora Conferences: Providing a platform for Jharkhandis living across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane to network and help one another.
Who it helps: The wider Jharkhandi and Adivasi diaspora in Maharashtra, reinforcing cultural roots, identity, and shared brotherhood.

Grounded Realities

Stories of Hope and Transformation

Real experiences illustrating how prompt community intervention changes trajectories for migrant families in Mumbai.

Crisis Intervention Case #24-M

Dignified Repatriation for a Stranded Family from Giridih

The Challenge

A migrant laborer in suburban Mumbai suffered sudden fatal cardiac trauma. With no immediate relatives in the state and high ambulance transport quotes, the grieving family was completely helpless.

How JPS Stepped In

JPS volunteers reached the civic hospital, completed administrative police & postmortem clearances, funded transit, and ensured the deceased was received with honor in their ancestral village.

"JPS stood beside us when we had nobody in Mumbai. They handled every paper, every formality, and brought our son home with dignity."

Child Protection Case #CWC-19

From Informal Labor Back to School: Rohit's New Beginning

The Challenge

An 11-year-old child of a construction worker had dropped out of 5th standard due to extreme poverty, spending days collecting scrap material to support household meals.

The JPS Resolution

In coordination with Child Welfare authorities, JPS counseled the parents, provided a full academic starter kit, and facilitated admission into a local municipal school with periodic mentor visits.

"Rohit now attends class every morning and dreams of becoming a school teacher. His school bag and notebooks gave him confidence."

Elder Healthcare #BW-72

Preventing Silent Stroke: Timely Care for Elder Shrimati Devi

The Challenge

A 68-year-old grandmother in Borivali West was enduring severe dizziness and blurred vision, unable to afford private physician checkups or diagnostic lab fees.

The JPS Resolution

At our monthly Free Senior Health Camp, doctors detected dangerously elevated blood pressure (195/110) and provided immediate stabilization, prescribed medicine course, and lifestyle guidance.

"The doctors treated me with as much respect as their own mother. My health is restored and I can walk to the temple peacefully."

Administrative Dialogue

Direct Representation Before Civic Authorities

JPS does not only provide relief; we engage directly with governance. In 2023 and 2024, JPS delegations formally met with the Deputy Commissioner / District Magistrate in Giridih and local Mumbai authorities to advocate for:

  • ✓ Mandatory registration desks for inter-state migrant labor.
  • ✓ Fast-track compensation channels for workplace casualties.
  • ✓ Institutional coordination between Maharashtra and Jharkhand labor departments.
